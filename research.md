## Cash protection after 1996

- [T1] CBPP state AFDC/TANF-to-poverty ratios, two-year averages 1978–79 to 2022–23 (spreadsheet 2025-04-11, Internet Archive capture). Moderator source. All 48 contiguous states fell between 1995–96 and 2015–16.
- [T1] Welfare Rules Databook 2016 (OPRE 2017-82), Table L5: maximum monthly benefit for a family of three, July 1996 (AFDC) and July 2016 (TANF). Transcribed; printed 1996 mean $392 and median $377 reproduced; printed 2016 mean $442 and median $431 reproduced with Wisconsin excluded, as the table does.
- [T2] CRS R45966 (Falk, Ghavalyan, Carter, October 9, 2019): participation 81.6% (1995) to 26.6% (2016) (text before Figure 7); poverty gap reduction 27.3% (1995) to 5.7% (2016) (Table 4); fixed $16.5 billion block grant, 36% loss of value since 1997 (Summary); most AFDC families headed by single mothers who reported no work (Summary).
- [T2] Parolin 2021 (abstract): decline of cash assistance 1993–2016 decomposed into participation 52%, need 21%, benefit levels 27%.
- [T2] Bitler and Hoynes 2016 (abstract): TANF did not respond in the Great Recession.
- [T2] Blank 2002, Moffitt 2015 (abstracts): broader record of welfare reform and of the redistribution of means-tested spending.
